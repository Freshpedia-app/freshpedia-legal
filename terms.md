## What the app is - and is not

Freshpedia provides **general educational information** about selecting and storing food: what fresh produce, meat, and fish tend to look like, common signs of decline, and typical storage guidance.

The app is **not** a food-safety service, and nothing in it is professional, medical, nutritional, or food-safety advice.

Please understand what this means in practice:

- The checks and cues in the app can improve your odds of picking good food. **They are not a guarantee.** Food that passes every visual check can still be spoiled or unsafe, and food handling before and after purchase is outside anyone's control but yours and the seller's.
- Storage times in the app are typical ranges, not promises. Your fridge, your kitchen, and the condition of the item when you bought it all change the real answer.
- The app cannot see, smell, or touch your food. **Your own judgment at the counter and at home always comes first.**
- For authoritative food-safety guidance, rely on official sources such as the USDA (fsis.usda.gov) and FDA (fda.gov/food), and when in doubt, throw it out.

Guide photos are AI-generated illustrations of real freshness cues, verified against reference photography.
They depict typical examples, not the exact item in front of you.
