# Know what to buy.

Standing in front of thirty avocados with no idea which one is ready is a small problem that happens every week. Freshpedia is the guide that answers it - for produce, meat, and fish.

## What it does

**113 guides, written for the aisle.** Each one tells you what to look for, what to press, what to smell, and what to walk away from. Thirty seconds, not an article.

**Three stages for produce, two for the counter.** Unripe, ripe, past it - with photographs of what each one actually looks like. Meat and fish get the only two answers that matter: take it home, or walk away.

**Check it.** Point your camera at something and Freshpedia tells you what it is and how it looks. Identifying the item works offline, on your phone.

**Recipes that start at the store.** Seasonal, and keyed to what you just learned to pick.

## Who makes it

Freshpedia is built by a small independent team. We do not collect personal information, we do not run ads, and we do not track what you read - the guides are on your phone, not on a server. The [privacy policy](privacy) says exactly what the app does and does not do, in plain language.
